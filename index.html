Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts on booking, logistics, sea seasons, packing, and conservation permits for Misool.

Every booking is handled directly through WhatsApp. After you send an inquiry, we discuss your dates, group size, ferry arrival schedule into Misool, and current weather. Once the details suit you, your spot is confirmed personally with me. There are no automated gateway payments or hidden middleman fees.
Yes, absolutely! If you only need a comfortable, trusted overwater village homestay to relax on your own, I will coordinate the reservation directly with local Papuan hosts and help ensure boat pickup from the harbor is arranged.
The prime season for Misool is typically from October through April, when the southern Raja Ampat seas are calmer, boat rides are smooth, and underwater visibility is crystal clear. Between June and August, seasonal winds can bring larger waves in open sea channels. Since weather conditions can fluctuate year to year, always message me on WhatsApp to ask about current, real-time sea conditions before booking your flights.
We strongly recommend packing:
  • Reef-safe biodegradable sunscreen and UV protection rash guard
  • A sturdy dry bag (15L–20L) for boat trips
  • Your own snorkel mask and fins if you prefer your personal fit
  • Waterproof phone pouch or underwater action camera
  • Mosquito repellent and personal prescription medications
  • Sufficient Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) cash: There are NO ATM machines or card terminals on the remote islands of Misool. Please withdraw cash in Sorong before taking the ferry.
No. The Raja Ampat Environmental Service Fee (Tarif Layanan Pemeliharaan Lingkungan / Kartu Jasa Lingkungan BLUD) is an official conservation levy paid directly to the local conservation management authority. It is not included in our tour fee, but I will gladly provide step-by-step guidance on how and where to purchase your valid pass before or upon arrival in Sorong.
